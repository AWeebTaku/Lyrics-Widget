# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful. This project is for people who like music on their desktop.

- Treat everyone with respect. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Keep issues and pull requests on the subject, and assume good faith.
- Do not publish other people's private information.

Maintainers may edit, close or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who
keep breaking them. To report a problem, use the repository's private
[Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/AWeebTaku/Lyrics-Widget/security/advisories/new) form and say it is about conduct.

This is a short version of the ideas in the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
